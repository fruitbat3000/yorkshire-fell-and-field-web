About

Two friends, one long-term idea.

Yorkshire Fell & Field began with a simple thought: what if the things we love doing outdoors could become a place other people could share?

Yorkshire, dry-stone walls and all.

Our story

Founded by two lifelong friends.

We’ve spent years enjoying the outdoors together — the early starts, the long walks, the food that tastes better for being earned. Yorkshire Fell & Field is our way of sharing that with other people.

It’s a long-term project, built patiently and alongside the rest of our lives. That’s deliberate: we’d rather grow something well than something quickly, and we’d rather be honest about being at the start than pretend we’re further along.

Open well, not early.

Why Yorkshire

Because it’s where we love to be. The fells, the farmland, the big sky and the honest, no-nonsense hospitality of the place shape everything we want to do here.

Where we are now

  • Building our foundations in Yorkshire
  • Opening in stages, fieldsports and country skills first
  • Not yet taking bookings
  • Keen to hear from anyone interested

What we care about

The way we’ll run things.

  • Safety & responsibility

    We take safety and the law seriously, and will never cut a corner to make something easier.

  • Stewardship

    The land, the wildlife and the neighbours come first. We want to leave things better than we found them.

  • Good food, honestly told

    Where food comes from matters, and so does telling the truth about it.

  • Welcome for everyone

    Beginners, families and old hands alike — with activities suited to each.

Come and say hello.
