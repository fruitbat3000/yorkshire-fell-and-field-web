Who we are

A country business, built slowly on purpose.

Yorkshire Fell & Field was started by two lifelong friends with a shared love of the outdoors.

We’re building a place where people can learn real country skills, spend a night under canvas, gather for a celebration, and sit down to food they can trace back to the field it came from.

It’s a long-term project, and we’re doing it carefully — safety, respect for the land and a warm welcome first, and everything else in its own time. We’re not taking bookings yet, and we’ll always be plain about what is available now and what is still on its way.

How we’re opening

How we work

What we hold to.

  • Safety first

    Proper briefings, small groups and strict respect for the law and for best practice.

  • Respect for the land

    We’re guests of the countryside as much as hosts. We leave it as good as we found it, or better.

  • Real skills, good food

    Things worth learning, and food worth sitting down to — without fuss or showing off.

  • Family-friendly

    A welcoming place for all ages, with activities suited to each. Age limits will be clear from the start.

Want to hear when we open?

We’re opening in stages and we’d love to keep you in the loop. Tell us what you’re interested in and we’ll be in touch when there’s something real to share.
