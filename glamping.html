Glamping & Stays

Sleep under canvas, wake up to the fell.

A yurt stay on our own Yorkshire land: an unhurried night away, with all the countryside and none of the tent-pegs.

Canvas, timber and a very big sky.

In development  Not yet open for booking. We’re preparing our land for yurt stays now. There are no dates or prices yet — register your interest and we’ll tell you first.

The idea

Somewhere to properly slow down.

A yurt is a lovely thing: round, canvas-and-timber, and somehow calmer than a room with corners. We’re building our glamping around that feeling — a comfortable place to sleep, a fire to sit around, and a big sky at the end of the day.

Our aim is simple comfort rather than showy luxury: good bedding, warm textiles, and a setting that does most of the work for us. We’ll share exactly what’s included, and how a stay works, once we’re nearer to opening.

Stay & do

Because we’re a countryside business first, a stay is designed to pair well with everything else we do: a morning of country skills, an evening of open-fire cooking, or a taste of field to fork.

What to expect from us

  • A yurt on our own land in Yorkshire
  • An unhurried, family-friendly welcome
  • Activities you can add to your stay
  • Honest information — no surprises
