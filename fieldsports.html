Fieldsports

Shooting, done thoughtfully.

Fieldsports are at their best when they’re about craft, safety and stewardship of the land — not trophies. Our approach is small groups, clear briefings, and plenty of patience for beginners.

How we approach fieldsports

  • Safety and legality first. Every session begins with a proper briefing, and we work strictly within the law and recognised codes of good practice.
  • Respect for wildlife and the land. Humane, responsible practice, with the countryside’s wellbeing ahead of anything else.
  • Clear eligibility. Age, experience and licensing requirements will be spelled out for each activity before you book.
